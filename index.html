Designing products, finance and processes that work for the people they serve.

I’m Arnelit Philip Mani. I work across inclusive finance, product design and enterprise development, with a research and advisory background in gender, agriculture and rural livelihoods. I look at how products, data and processes are designed, and whether they work for the people they are meant to serve.

What I work on

Four areas that feed into each other.

Inclusive finance and financial systems

  • Women’s financial inclusion and digital adoption
  • Sex-disaggregated data in financial institutions
  • Inclusive financial products and credit design
  • Enterprise and customer financing

Inclusive design and enterprise development

  • Designing products and processes around the average woman at the margins
  • Women’s participation in enterprise value chains
  • Gender-responsive business models and go-to-market
  • Persona-based research and field tools

Agriculture, livelihoods and climate resilience

  • Asset and green finance for smallholder farmers
  • Risk financing and parametric insurance
  • Livelihoods of women in informal and rural work
  • Climate risk market infrastructure

Research, strategy and advisory

  • Market and ecosystem research
  • Field research and evidence synthesis
  • Technical assistance for enterprises
  • Turning findings into decisions

A repayment schedule, an OTP screen and a rainfall trigger are design choices that decide who gets served.
